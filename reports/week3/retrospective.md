# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## What went well

1. The team did a good job at breaking down user stories into actionable PBIs. Each team member knew what they needed to do to fully implement MVP v1 during this sprint.
2. The team adapted to customer feedback. After the customer review, we added US-012: Basic Password Protection to the Sprint 1 scope, even though this wasn't originally planned.
3. We kept our Github workflow highly organized. All issues can be traced to their related PRs, which are checked by fellow team members.

## What did not go well

1. It was difficult to begin working at the beginning of the sprint. Team members took 1-2 days to write all of their PBIs, but after that we were able to speed up and complete all of our work.
2. Because all "Must Have" stories were targeted for MVP v1, the entire Product Backlog was pulled into Sprint 1. This resulted in an unusually large Sprint Backlog and left the remaining future PBIs unestimated.

## Action points

1. Dedicate time at the beginning of Sprint 2 to properly estimate the remaining "Should Have" and "Could Have" PBIs to ensure a balanced sprint capacity and clearer roadmap.
2. Ensure the team has a clear vision of the deliverable at the beginning of the next sprint. We have some PBIs left over that describe improvements to currently implemented user stories. It will be important that we minimize scope creep and implement what is actually important to the customer.
